Anatomy of a click

Why this thumbnail works

Four of the signals ThumbAI measures, shown on a real frame. No face, no focal point, no contrast — no click.

1 2 3 4
1

An expression you can read at 2 cm

Wide eyes, open mouth, head turned toward the object. On a phone feed the face is the size of a fingernail — subtle expressions disappear.

2

One glowing hero object

A single subject with a light effect around it. The pointing hand builds a literal arrow from the face to the product — the eye follows it for free.

3

Two colors, maximum fight

Red and yellow sit opposite on the thumbnail battlefield: they stay separable even in peripheral vision while scrolling.

4

Dead space reserved for text

The empty left third is not wasted — it is where three huge words go. Busy backgrounds behind text kill readability and the click with it.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is the score real, or made up?

Real — it is computed from your image, never invented. The Studio measures saturation, contrast spread, sharpness, the size and position of the face region, clean text space and clutter in your actual pixels, and turns those measurements into the score and the fix list. If you paste a vision API key into assets/app.js (the THUMBAI_API block at the very top), the same upload also gets a deep AI read — subject, expression and any text in the frame — and the results panel tells you which engine produced your numbers.

Which image generators accept the prompts?

All of them. Universal format works in Nano Banana, DALL·E and Firefly as-is; Midjourney format adds the aspect-ratio and style parameters Midjourney expects; Stable Diffusion format adds a negative prompt. Pick the format in the Studio before you copy.

Can I use ThumbAI to win design clients?

That is one of its main jobs. Run a prospect's latest thumbnail through the Studio, download the audit report, and send it with your redesign pitch — a scored, specific critique opens doors that “I make thumbnails” never will. Audits are free and unlimited right now, so run one for every prospect on your list.

Do my uploads get stored anywhere?

Not by this page. By default, analysis happens entirely in your browser and the image is gone when you close the tab. The one exception: if you connect your own vision API key in assets/app.js, the image is sent to that provider for the deep read, under their privacy policy — and the results panel says when the AI engine was the one that ran.

What makes a thumbnail score high?

The big ones, in roughly this order: a face or single focal subject readable at tiny size, two or three colors fighting at full saturation, dark–light contrast that survives a squint, a clean zone for three or fewer huge words, sharpness that holds up after YouTube's compression, and a frame clean enough that nothing competes for the half-second of attention. The Studio measures every one of them.
