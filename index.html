Questions

Common questions

How does the score work?

Simple: the tool looks at your picture and checks the things people notice in the first half-second — bright colors, a clear face or one main object, dark and light areas that stand apart, empty space for big text, and how sharp and uncluttered it looks. Those checks become one score out of 100, and anything weak comes with the fix written next to it.

What is a "prompt"?

A prompt is the sentence you give an AI image tool (like Midjourney or Nano Banana) to tell it what picture to make. Upload a photo here and ThumbAI writes that sentence for you, based on the photo's subject, colors and lighting — copy it, paste it into the tool, and you get a similar picture.

Is it really free?

Yes — right now everything on this page is free to use, as much as you want. No account, no card, nothing to install.

Is my picture uploaded or saved anywhere?

No. Your picture is checked inside your own browser, on your phone or computer, and it is gone when you close this page.

What makes a thumbnail get a high score?

A big, clear face or one main object people can recognize when the thumbnail is tiny, two or three bright colors, a background that stands apart from the subject, space for three big words or fewer, and nothing extra cluttering the picture.
